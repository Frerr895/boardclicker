How scoring works

Timed Challenge

Your score is set by a multiplication of difficulty*amount of letters clicked*distance between checkpoints. Retrying after a checkpoint doesn't cost you points.

Difficulty

Higher difficulties are worth more: Easy multiplies by 1, Medium by 1.6, Hard by 2.3 and Insane by 3. They are based on how much faster and how much larger the letter pool gets.

Checkpoints

Same thing with the Checkpoints, the further away they are from each other, the more they multiply, because a single mistake can cost you more progress. Chekpoint every 15s multpilies by 0.85, every 30s multiplies by 1, every 60s by 1.3 and Hardcore (one mistake resets everything) multiplies by 2.

Survival

There's no difficulty to pick, so each letter is scored individually by how fast the pace was when you typed it. The mathematical operation for the score of each letter is 2000/the actual pace, and in the end all the individual letter scores are added together.

Speed Test

Speed Test isn't part of this points system, it has its own leaderboard based on letters per minute instead.
